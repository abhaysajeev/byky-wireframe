Charged once the package time plus grace period has elapsed.

How the charge is calculated. Package time plus grace equals the basic fare. Every completed concurrent interval after that adds the concurrent price. For example 30 min with 5 min grace is AED 100; at 38 minutes one 10-minute slab is added, giving AED 110.
